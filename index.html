InformlyConcept prototype

About this prototype

An independent product concept, not an existing Informly feature. It shows how a short, optional, topic-related mini-game could introduce a survey.

Problem explored

People often skip surveys because they feel repetitive or dull. Could a brief, skippable warm-up make the start feel friendlier?

Journey

  • Welcome: play a game or skip straight to the survey
  • Game: best of three rounds, picking one of three cards each time (about 15 seconds). Two are open, one is locked. Win or lose, the survey is identical. Only the wording changes.
  • Survey: each answer opens a lock on Bo’s vault. A small, purely fun surprise waits at the end.
  • Transition, then a 5-question survey, then thanks

To test with real participants

  • Completion and drop-off versus a standard intro (A/B)
  • Whether the game feels like a barrier, and how many skip
  • Effect on answer quality or bias, including whether the “forfeit” framing or vault progress feels pushy or manipulative
  • Whether losing feels playful rather than punishing, and which win/lose odds feel fair
  • Time added, accessibility, and fit across topics and audiences

No claim is made that this improves engagement. That needs experimental evidence. Answers stay in your browser tab and are not sent anywhere.
